Production delivery

Samples and gap events are sent to the broker. QoS 1 waits for broker confirmation; QoS 0 may lose messages after the client sends them. Consumers should deduplicate using the stable IDs in JSON v1.

Every record still pending in the local spool follows the latest saved destination and QoS. Records already acknowledged remain at their prior destination; changing destinations can create duplicates across targets.

Historical samples and downstream health belong to your external consumer. DAQNavi shows its acquisition, local spool, gaps, and broker delivery state only.
